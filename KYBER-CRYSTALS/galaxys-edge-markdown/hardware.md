# Hardware

Documenting hardware details from Galaxy's Edge devices.

## Screws

### General Notes

Most of these screws are self-tapping screws, sometimes referred to as "sheet metal" screws. These have a coarser thread pitch than machine screws.

The self-tapping screws we can find will have a sharp tip, but the ones used in manufacturing will have a blunt tip. You can leave the sharp tip and it may dig into the plastic a little further, or you can trim it off.

### Screw Specifications

| Component | Screw Specs | Compatible Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| BB Motivator Shell | 0.66" long, 0.12" diameter, 24 TPI, phillips, pan head | #4, 3/4" long, phillips pan head sheet metal screw |
| Savi's Chassis | 0.34" long, 0.12" diameter, 24 TPI, hex, flat head | M3-12 or longer (trim as needed). A #5 wood screw or #4 will work but need trimming. |

## Savi's Chassis Thread Specifications

| Part | Thread Spec |
|---|---|
| Emitter | M39 with 2mm pitch |
| Pommel | M40 with 1.5mm pitch |
