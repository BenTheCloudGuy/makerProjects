# Holocron Quotes

A comprehensive list of quotes from Galaxy's Edge Holocrons, Series 1 and 2.

## About Series 2 Holocrons & Kyber Crystals

On May 4, 2024 the series 2 holocrons were released. These holocrons contain all the audio from series 1 as well as new audio for series 2 kyber crystals.

- Series 1 and 2 kyber crystals work with series 2 holocrons, however series 2 audio quotes will only play with series 2 kyber crystals.
- Series 2 crystals will work in a series 1 holocron, but will only play series 1 audio.

### What Do The Track Numbers Mean?

Most of the audio played through holocrons is stored in a memory chip inside the holocron. The contents of these chips were extracted and analyzed to identify the total number of tracks.

The track number refers to the placement of the audio data for a given quote within that memory chip. Series 2 holocrons contain quotes for both holocrons (e.g., dark side crystal audio exists in the Jedi holocron but is never used). As a result, only quotes accessible for the given holocron are listed, so track numbers will have gaps.
